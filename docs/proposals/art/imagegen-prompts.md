## 공통 프롬프트

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original browser game art-direction keyframe, 16:9 landscape
Primary request: Create a polished top-down three-quarter-view gameplay concept image for an original squad-command survival roguelite. Show one central commander controlling two small autonomous squads, one squad marked teal and the other vermilion, surrounded by a much larger enemy horde. Include one clearly downed friendly soldier with a readable rescue indicator made only from shape and light, and a distinct enemy commander deeper in the crowd. The scene must communicate movement-only command, automatic combat, squad switching, fatigue, rescue, and enemy morale collapse through visuals.
Composition/framing: wide battlefield, player forces readable at center, enemy pressure from several directions, clear silhouettes and uncluttered gameplay lanes, polished game concept art rather than a poster
Constraints: fully original characters, world, weapons, symbols and interface language; no resemblance to named game characters or proprietary UI; no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark; accessible color separation; gameplay readability is more important than decorative detail.
Avoid: copyrighted characters, Vampire Survivors-specific imagery, First Queen-specific imagery, photorealistic gore, excessive UI panels, illegible clutter.
```
